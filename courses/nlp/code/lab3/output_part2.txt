Step 3: Results and Discussion
============================================================
Observations:

1. Gender Bias in Word Embeddings:
   The analogies reveal gender biases present in the training data.
   For example, 'computer_programmer - man + woman' may yield
   gender-stereotyped results rather than 'computer_programmer'.

2. Analogy Quality:
   'King - Man + Woman = Queen' is the classic well-known analogy
   that word embeddings handle well. Other analogies may produce
   less intuitive results due to biases in training corpora.

3. Sub-word Advantage:
   FastText can compute vectors for compound words like
   'computer_programmer' using character n-grams, even if the
   exact token was rare in training data.

Summary:
  Word2Vec cannot handle misspelled words that are not in its
  vocabulary, returning N/A. FastText leverages sub-word (character
  n-gram) information to generate meaningful vectors even for
  misspelled words, resulting in high similarity scores between
  correct and misspelled variants.
